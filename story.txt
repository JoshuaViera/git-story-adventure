The old lighthouse had been dark for decades, but tonight, a rhythmic amber pulse flickered from the lantern room. Elias stood on the shore, gripping his radio as he watched the light signal a code he hadn't heard since the war.

He didn't wait for a response on the frequency and began sprinting toward the jagged cliffs. As he reached the rusted iron door, it creaked open perfectly, as if the building itself had been expecting his arrival.

The ghostly hands paused their typing and pointed toward a dusty glass jar sitting on the workbench. Inside the jar, a miniature storm was brewing, complete with tiny lightning bolts that illuminated a folded note at the bottom.
